Laura Radics
As an architect, photography has become a natural extension of my perspective, allowing me to explore how light transforms a space, how people interact with their surroundings and how small details contribute to the experience of a place. It is also a way of preserving moments and the memories attached to them.
Through photography, I explore the same questions that guide my architectural work: how spaces are perceived, experienced, and remembered.
