Laura Radics
As an architect currently based in Basel, I have developed an interest in photography alongside my architectural practice. Photography has become a natural extension of my perspective, allowing me to explore how light transforms a space, how people interact with their surroundings, and how small details contribute to the experience of a place. Through photography, I explore the same questions that guide my architectural work: how spaces are perceived, experienced, and remembered.
